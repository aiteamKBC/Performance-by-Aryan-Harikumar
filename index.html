All 30 learners have completed hours, minimum required hours, planned ILR hours and a gateway date read from Aptem. Plans default to the Aptem minimum. Submitted hours are shown separately and do not reduce the outstanding completed-hour total. Confirm the agreed target with the coach.

How the red, amber and green categories work

Red flags insufficient agreed weekly time, no time left before gateway, or a college OTJH status of At Risk when weekly time is not agreed. Amber flags missing or invalid plan details, or Need Attention while weekly time is not agreed. Green means the selected hours goal is met, agreed weekly time covers the required pace, or the college status is On Track while weekly time still needs confirmation. These are planning categories, separate from the college’s OTJH labels. Changes to hours, dates, weeks away or agreed capacity recalculate the category. The progress bar shows completed hours against the selected goal.
